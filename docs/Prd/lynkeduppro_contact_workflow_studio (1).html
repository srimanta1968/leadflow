Decision Principles

Identity AI proposes; deterministic rules and humans govern high-risk truth.

Link, do not overwrite

Both source records survive with provenance, effective dates and crosswalks. The display projection applies survivorship rules.

Risk-tiered automation

Exact source crosswalks and safe deterministic matches may auto-link. Ambiguous names, phones or properties become cases.

Reversible by design

A verified link can be retracted, projections replayed, and the affected audit trail reconstructed.
