ACCOUNTABILITY
The queue is a team responsibility; the record is owned by one 
named person.
ROLE OWNS MUST NOT DELEGATE
Sales Rep / SDR First response, call outcomes, qualification, 
follow-up, booking, CRM notes. Accurate disposition and NEXT.
Backup Rep Takes over when the primary is unavailable or 
near breach. Immediate accept/decline with reason.
Sales Manager SLA board, coverage, coaching, reassignment, 
stale pipeline. Same-day recovery of breaches/orphans.
Revenue Operations Routing, dedup, sequences, templates, 
dashboards, provider health. System integrity and rollback.
Leadership Offer truth, staffing, claims, incentives, executive 
escalation. No false urgency or compliance bypass.
Client Success / Onboarding Closed-won intake, kickoff scheduling, readiness, 
promise check. Acknowledgement of every handoff.
Coverage standard
TIME / EVENT PRIMARY ACTION CONTROL
8:45 a.m. CT Opening rep validates phone, email, SMS, 
calendar, and clears overnight queue. Manager confirms coverage.
9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. CT Thirty-minute SLA applies to qualifying inbound 
leads. Configured as America/Chicago, not fixed UTC-6.
4:30-5:30 p.m. CT Late coverage stays active so 4:59 p.m. leads 
receive the full response window. No early sign-off.

promise for an exit criterion.

HUMAN CALL DELIVERY AND 
OPENING
Use anchor language, not a robotic monologue. Sound calm, curious, 
and prepared.
ELEMENT DELIVERY STANDARD
Tone Warm confidence. Professional, fast, calm - never frantic or overly “salesy.”
Energy About 10% above the prospect; match pace without copying stress or 
negativity.
Pace Conversational. Slow down on important points and after questions.
Inflection Use a grounded downward finish on clear statements; avoid sounding 
unsure or sing-song.
Listening Aim to listen more than talk. Ask one question at a time and allow silence.
Language Use the prospect’s words. Avoid jargon, feature dumping, and “just 
checking in.”
Demeanor Smile before dialing, sit/stand tall, take notes, and sound genuinely pleased 
they raised a hand.
First call - permission and curiosity opener
Intent: Lower resistance, connect the call to the prospect’s action, 
and invite a real conversation.
Warm recognition: “Hi [First Name], this is [Rep] with Lynked Up 
Pro. You just [filled out the form / messaged us / started the 
Founders License checkout].”
Permission: “Did I catch you with two minutes?”
Curiosity - pause after this: “What caught your attention, or what 
was happening in the business that made you raise your hand 
today?”
Set an upfront contract
Intent: Make the conversation safe and mutual; remove the fear of a 
surprise pitch.
Set the frame: “I am not going to dump a software pitch on you. I 
will ask a few questions, tell you honestly where we fit and where 
we do not, and then we can either book a 30-minute demo, set a 
later follow-up, or close the loop. Fair?”
If they are busy: “Totally fair. Is your main interest lead follow-up, 
project visibility, or the prelaunch license? Great - let’s lock a 
better time. Is morning or afternoon easier?”
Do not read word-for-word: Keep the intent and structure. Use natural contractions, pauses, and the prospect’s language. The best call 
sounds like a prepared human, not a recited script.

OBJECTION HANDLING I: 
CLARIFY BEFORE RESPONDING
Listen > Label > Explore > Respond > Confirm > Schedule.
TACTICAL EMPATHY
Do not “overcome” the buyer. Name the concern without 
judgment, ask a calibrated question, answer only the real 
issue, and confirm what remains.
OBJECTION HUMAN RESPONSE NEXT
“It costs too much.”
“It sounds like the investment feels ahead of the 
value right now. Compared with what - budget 
available, alternatives, or the cost of the current 
problem?” Then quantify the impact using the 
prospect’s own facts; never invent ROI.
Reconfirm value, payment path, or close/nurture.
“We already have a CRM.”
“That makes sense. I would not replace a working 
system without a reason. Where does it still break 
between lead, follow-up, field proof, production, or 
owner visibility?”
Define the gap; demo only the gap.
“Send me information.”
“Happy to. So I send something useful, which issue 
should it help you evaluate? And when should we 
spend 15 minutes deciding whether it is relevant?”
Send tailored asset + calendar.
“I need to think about it.”
“Of course. Usually that means fit, timing, money, 
trust, or another person. Which one deserves the 
thinking?”
Book decision event around the real issue.
“I need my partner/team.”
“Absolutely. What will they care about most, and what 
would make the next conversation productive for

Book with stakeholders; add agenda.
